Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a new primary desktop application icon for Fusion Studio, a community desktop toolkit integrating MITRE ATT&CK and MITRE ATLAS in one shared Workplace for threat research and attack/defense planning.
Input image role: the existing blue pixel shield icon is a style/color reference only. Redesign the internal symbol for the product's expanded ATT&CK plus ATLAS identity rather than copying the old hierarchy/checkmark.
Primary request: one memorable unified pixel-art icon that fuses a protective cyber shield, connected technique nodes, and an AI microchip into one coherent mark.
Subject design: a bold stepped blue shield silhouette with an opaque dark-charcoal interior. Inside it, three substantial connected square technique nodes on the left flow into one larger square processor on the right, with just three broad short chip pins on its right edge. The nodes and processor must share a single thick continuous circuit path, visually conveying two knowledge frameworks brought together. Give the processor a small near-white central core, not text or a brain illustration. Balance the two elements and keep their geometry extremely simple. Omit the old checkmark so the fusion symbol has enough space.
Style/medium: refined authentic pixel art, chunky square clusters and deliberate staircase edges on a consistent low-resolution logical grid, about 48x48 logical pixels enlarged. Match the reference's blue/charcoal palette, dark-blue outlines and small light-blue/white highlights. Use a restrained palette with flat pixel shading; no smooth curves or gradient rendering.
Color palette: dark outline #003b78, strong blue #007acc, brighter blue #149dff, light cyan-blue #66d4ff, charcoal #1e1e1e, near-white #f2f9ff. This is the project's own visual language, not official framework branding.
Composition: square canvas, one centered cohesive icon occupying roughly 80-85% of the canvas, consistent transparent margin. Large bold outer contour, inner symbols separated by generous negative space; recognizable at 32px and 16px.
Background: actual transparent alpha outside the icon, with dark interior opaque. All transparency must be genuine, not a checkerboard illustration.
Constraints: one final app icon only; no text, no lettering, no wordmark, no mockup, no presentation board, no multiple alternatives, no watermark, no official MITRE/ATLAS logos, no padlock, no hacker face, no tiny decorative circuits, no particles, no external glow, no shadow outside silhouette, no photorealistic or 3D finish.
